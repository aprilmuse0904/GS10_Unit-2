Passive Voice · Câu bị động

Use the passive when the action or its receiver matters more than the doer. Đưa tân ngữ (O) của câu chủ động lên làm chủ ngữ của câu bị động.

Three-step method
1. Find S – V – O in the active sentence.
2. Identify the tense and sentence type (affirmative / negative / question).
3. Move O to the front; choose the correct form of be + V3. Keep time expressions and meaning.
Active: People recycle paper. → Passive: Paper is recycled (by people).

“By + doer” may be omitted when the doer is general or unimportant. “By” phrases are sometimes optional in the model answers. The text checker is deliberately forgiving with punctuation, letter case and optional by-phrases shown in brackets.

Final task · Present a greener school plan

Presentation structure: Opening → Club activities → Green living advice → Carbon footprint → Closing

Guided planning

Speaking checklist

Model:

Good morning, everyone! Imagine a school where every classroom is clean and every student cares about the planet. Today, I would like to share our green school plan.

First, a Go Green Club is going to be set up. Clean-up activities will be organised every Friday, so the playground will be cleaner. More trees will also be planted.

At home, we should turn off appliances that are not in use, choose reusable bags and sort our rubbish. These activities reduce waste and save energy.

Our daily activities produce CO₂. Too many emissions can lead to increasing global temperatures. We can reduce our carbon footprint by walking, cycling and using public transport.

Together, we can make our school and community greener. Thank you for listening!
